## Industry Buzz

**Pace Principle 2.0: social and creator marketing face three structural ceilings**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/pace-principle-2-social-and-creator-marketing-face-three-structural-ceilings-3aed7a59a2db4a368a663604b8814131), Aug 4 2026
- **What:** Across 210 SE Asia/Greater China/India case studies, broad emotional campaigns outperformed broad social ones 2.1x on brand and business impact — social/creator marketing alone caps out on a platform ceiling, a cultural ceiling, and a "self-sustaining" ceiling.
- **Why us:** Doesn't kill the social-first pitch, but sharpens it — the answer is social-first *plus* broad emotional reach, not social-first *instead of* it. Worth a proactive point of view before a client brings this to us first.

**How brands can restructure content to increase visibility on LLMs**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/how-brands-can-restructure-content-to-increase-visibility-on-llms-fa776f2f235240ee93edb2d504ade444), Aug 7 2026
- **What:** Clearer content structure (headings, short paragraphs, direct-answer lists) improves LLM citation rates — but roughly two-thirds of a brand's existing LLM visibility already traces to years-old brand equity, not anything published recently.
- **Why us:** A hard number for "compound, don't hoard" applied to AEO specifically: the C-suite AI-visibility question isn't just a content-formatting fix, it's another argument for sustained brand-building over a one-off AEO push.

**SCMP redesigns print and digital for an AI-first era**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`

- **Why us:** A publisher restructuring its whole product — not just its copy — around an AI-mediated discovery environment; a live case study for AEO-as-product-strategy conversations, not just AEO-as-content-tweak.

**McDonald's rethinks its promo calendar after a crowded FIFA World Cup tie-in underperforms**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
