## Industry Buzz

**ChatGPT Ads: $1B run rate, SEA launch, "sponsored agents"**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Interactive — ChatGPT Ads makes its Southeast Asia debut](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/chatgpt-ads-makes-its-southeast-asia-debut), Sept 24 2026 (also [Digiday — Here's what OpenAI needs to do to reach its billion-dollar ad ambitions](https://digiday.com/podcasts/heres-what-openai-needs-to-do-to-reach-its-billion-dollar-ad-ambitions/), Sept 29 2026)
- **What:** Ads now run on free and standard plans in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan. Alongside standard units, "sponsored agents" let users chat with a business in-conversation before clicking out. OpenAI is targeting $25B in ad revenue by the end of 2026 and $100B by 2030; buyers cite attribution gaps, restrictive T&Cs and thin inventory.
- **Why us:** This is where AEO and paid meet. Last week's story was organic citation share. Now there's a paid layer on the same surface, and "sponsored agents" is a conversational format that needs a brand voice, not a banner. Worth an APAC-first POV on how to show up in chat, paid and earned, before clients get sold a media plan alone.

**AI answer engines misidentify a former Converse CMO, and the threats are real**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Former CMO Rodney Rambo Had Nothing To Do With Converse's Controversial Ad. He Still Got Death Threats.](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/former-cmo-rodney-rambo-had-nothing-to-do-with-converses-controversial-ad-he-still-got-death-threats/), Sept 29 2026
- **What:** After backlash to a Converse ad with aespa's Karina, AI answer engines named Rambo as responsible, though he'd left in January. He was doxxed and threatened, and got help mainly through his own professional network.
- **Why us:** Adds a new side to the AEO conversation: it isn't only about being cited, it's about being cited *correctly*. During a crisis, answer engines can put a named executive at the centre of the story. Social listening and crisis playbooks now need to cover what the engines are saying, not just the feeds.

**CMO tenure is down to 2.6 years for new starters**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — The Rundown: Average CMO tenure has shrunk by over a third in 16 years](https://digiday.com/marketing/the-rundown-average-cmo-tenure-has-shrunk-by-over-a-third-in-16-years/), Sept 29 2026
- **What:** A Findem/CMO Huddles study of 13,000 US marketers finds median CMO tenure fell 35% since 2010, from four years to 2.6 years for those starting in 2022 or later. Only 33% stay beyond five years; software CMOs average 27 months.
- **Why us:** Complicates the compounding argument. Patient brand-building has to outlast the person who signs it off. It's a strong framing for why the agency relationship (and a codified POV like the Loop) should be the continuity layer, and a useful stat for Think Forward.

**e.l.f. releases its second album through its entertainment arm**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — E.l.f. jams to second music album under entertainment marketing arm](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/elf-jams-to-second-music-album-under-entertainment-marketing-arm/831453/), Sept 28 2026
- **What:** "Mirror Mix," from e.l.f. Made, features seven emerging artists and is promoted through Genius's Open Mic series and the brand's Roblox experience. The first album drew 500K+ Spotify streams and 10M views.
- **Why us:** Second retailer/brand in two briefs building a standing entertainment function (after Gap's "Fashiontainment"). Entertainment as reserve currency is becoming an operating model, not a campaign, and this is a beauty example for the Q2 compounding thread.

**Hoka runs the NYC Marathon build-up as a community play**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Behind Hoka's bid to own the NYC marathon's run-up period](https://digiday.com/marketing/behind-hokas-bid-to-own-the-nyc-marathons-run-up-period/), Sept 29 2026 (corroborated: [Highsnobiety — AG1 Opened Berlin's Buzziest Bar for Marathon Weekend](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/ag1-bar-berlin-marathon/), Sept 29)
- **What:** Paid social on TikTok and Meta, six creator partners, digital billboards tracking a borough-by-borough Strava challenge, and a block party, framed as talking about the community rather than advertising Hoka. Deckers' marketing spend is up 13% YoY.

**Publishers lobby for a federal "bad bots" bill**  ·  `SIGNAL`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Condé Nast, Hearst among 300 media execs to push federal 'bad bots' bill on AI scraping](https://digiday.com/media/conde-nast-hearst-among-300-media-execs-to-push-federal-bad-bots-bill-on-ai-scraping/), Sept 29 2026
- **What:** The Stealth Bot Prohibition Act would force AI crawlers to identify themselves and their purpose, so publishers can block or charge them.
- **Why us:** Watch-line for AEO. If premium publishers wall off from answer engines, the sources engines cite will shift, and so will where brands need earned coverage.

## Trends

**"That's AI" and "LARPing": the new vocabulary of fakeness**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [After School — Beast Wearables and Trad Swiping](https://afterschool.substack.com/p/beast-wearables-and-trad-swiping), Sept 24 2026 (also [The New York Times — How LARPing, a Gaming Term, Became a Word for Posers](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/29/style/larp-slang-meaning-tiktok.html), Sept 29 2026, framing only)
- **What:** Gen Alpha uses "that's AI" to dismiss anything cheap, knockoff or overclaimed. On TikTok, "LARPing" has become the shorthand for calling someone out as performing an identity they don't hold.
- **Why us:** Unfakeable capital now has its own slang. Young audiences have a quick verdict for inauthenticity, and "AI" itself has become an insult. That's a sharp, quotable hook for the Q1 argument, and a caution for any brand leaning on visibly synthetic creative.

**Dupe culture gets more brazen**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Business of Fashion — The Politics of Duping](https://www.businessoffashion.com/articles/beauty/the-politics-of-duping/), Sept 29 2026 (corroborated: [After School — Swan Butts and Toddler Caviar](https://afterschool.substack.com/p/swan-butts-and-toddler-caviar), Sept 28; [Marketing Interactive — Brands put their spin on beauty's packaging déjà vu](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/brands-put-their-spin-on-beauty-s-packaging-deja-vu), Sept 28)
- **What:** Dupes are now an expected beauty category, with "superdupe" brands copying formulas outright. After School flags creators matching a $30 Walmart/Brandon Maxwell jean with Khaite's $740 style almost wash for wash.
- **Why us:** Second week running (after the Samhwa eye-mask story). When product and packaging are copyable at every price point, what's left is brand meaning and community, which is the Power arm. Good material for a luxury/beauty POV on what a premium is actually defending.

**"Motherbombing": young artists borrow enduring icons as attention insurance**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Dazed — Why everyone is 'motherbombing' right now](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/71054/1/everyone-motherbombing-right-now-madonna-charli-xcx-adela-nicole-kidman), Sept 28 2026
- **What:** Rising acts put established icons front and centre (Adéla's Nicole Kidman song and video, Charli xcx's cover with Scorsese, Marc Jacobs and John Cale, Troye Sivan sampling Madonna) to borrow credibility from people with lasting social capital.
- **Why us:** A cultural version of the compounding thesis: when virality fades fast, creators attach themselves to capital that has already compounded. For brands, it's a clear Proof mechanic, borrowing heritage deliberately, and it works best when the link is earned, not just bought.

## POVs

**Zoe Scaman: "The Identity Shift," the week the agent got a face**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Musings Of A Wandering Mind — The Identity Shift](https://zoescaman.substack.com/p/the-identity-shift), Sept 28 2026 — Zoe Scaman
- **What:** She revisits a talk from a year ago after Meta Connect (Sept 23), where Zuckerberg unveiled the Muse Charm, a keychain device with a friendly on-screen character, and Muse retail tie-ups. Our extension: social and creators are where that Proof gets documented, so what we build compounds into what agents read. It follows yesterday's Muse item and belongs in the Think Forward scope.
