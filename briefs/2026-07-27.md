## Industry Buzz

**K-culture graduates from fad to lifestyle category**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing-Interactive — Is this the end of the Korean wave?](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/is-this-the-end-of-the-korean-wave), 27 Jul 2026
- **What:** K-culture is maturing from an entertainment-driven fad into a durable lifestyle category — but "cultural relevance alone is no longer enough": brands need product performance, creator partnerships and market-specific localization to convert curiosity into habitual purchase.
- **Why us:** Direct proof that Presence alone doesn't compound — it needs earned Proof and creator-led Participation behind it; feeds the in-flight Creator Report (Aug) and sits squarely in Entertainment, a named priority category.

**New data quantifies the "churn and burn" problem**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [WARC — The trap of digital "churn and burn" strategies](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/the-trap-of-digital-churn-and-burn-strategies-31342727f7154d94bfabd2aa606f470a), 22 Jul 2026
- **What:** New CreativeX data across 633,000 ads: content production up 29%, media spend per ad down 15%, 93% of ads run on under $10k, and up to $100bn a year spent on content that's never published.
- **Why us:** Hard numbers behind what NOT compounding looks like at scale — corroborates last week's IPA Bellwether must-know and gives "don't hoard cultural capital, compound it" a concrete industry-wide waste figure; FMCG Report and Think Forward material.

**AI is reshaping SEA shopping, but trust still runs through people**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing-Interactive — AI is changing how SEA shops, but trust still wins the checkout](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/ai-is-changing-how-sea-shops-but-trust-still-wins-the-checkout), 24 Jul 2026
- **What:** 24% of SEA consumers now use generative AI in shopping decisions, but trusted people (family/friends, then creators at 36%) still outrank AI recommendations (25%) — AI is an added layer, not a replacement channel.
- **Why us:** Direct evidence for the AEO/AI-in-value-chain C-suite hot button and our "creators as trust infrastructure" argument — AI adoption doesn't dilute the case for creator partnerships, it raises the bar for them.

## Trends

**The AI backlash has a name, and it's analog**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [The Future Laboratory — Global Drivers 2027+: Colliding Technologies](https://www.thefuturelaboratory.com/blog/an-introduction-to-our-first-global-driver-2027-colliding-technologies), 22 Jul 2026
- **What:** The Future Laboratory's first Global Drivers report names AI/quantum/synbio convergence as 2027+'s defining force — and flags the direct cultural counter-reaction: surveillance anxiety, digital-sovereignty demands, and a turn back to analog experience. Same week, Ad Age's Thomas Kolster reviewed Polaroid's anti-tech "analog life" billboards, and After School reported Gen Z prizing unpolished "natural aegyo" over AI-perfected imagery.
- **Why us:** Three independent registries converging on one thesis in a single week is a whitespace signal, not consensus — "provably human" as unfakeable capital is the answer to AI backlash we're positioned to own; Think Forward (kicks off Oct) shaping material.

**Gen Z's craving for "natural aegyo" over AI polish**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [After School — Natural Aegyo and Artificial Idiots](https://afterschool.substack.com/p/natural-aegyo-and-artificial-idiots), 24 Jul 2026
- **What:** Gen Z is praising "natural aegyo" (visibly unpolished charm) as relief from AI-perfected imagery, while distrusting traditional ambition — the gym and extreme saving read as rare "controllable" domains in an unpredictable world.
- **Why us:** A fourth consecutive week After School has surfaced authenticity-craving behaviour — clears the rubric's own multi-week trend bar; live "unfakeable capital" proof point and Creator Report material on what reads as credible vs. synthetic talent.

**Institutional capital bets on the Gen Z "jam boom"**  ·  `SIGNAL`
- **Source:** [Feed Me — Why are young people eating so much jam?](https://www.readfeedme.com/p/why-are-young-people-eating-so-much), 24 Jul 2026
- **What:** LVMH's L Catterton backed an $18m round for a jam brand, treating a niche Gen Z food habit as a genuine investable category.
- **Why us:** Watch-line for the FMCG Report (Oct) — whether "compounding" cultural capital in food has become legible to institutional capital, not just brand marketers.

**Movie theaters trade as cheap, viable assets**  ·  `SIGNAL`
- **Source:** [Feed Me — Buy a movie theater for under $1 million](https://www.readfeedme.com/p/buy-a-movie-theater-for-under-1-million), 22 Jul 2026
- **What:** Independent cinemas are changing hands as surprisingly affordable, still-viable businesses even as the format is declared dead everywhere else.
- **Why us:** Second data point this month (after the Nolan "Odyssey" scarcity-sellout item logged 24 Jul) that theatrical exclusivity compounds value rather than eroding it — Entertainment category material.
