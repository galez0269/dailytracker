## Industry Buzz

**Publishers preparing to block Google's crawler over AI Overviews**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/publishers-opt-out-google-search/), Aug 4 2026
- **What:** Google's AI Overviews and search crawler share the same code, so publishers can't block one without vanishing from the other — and several (Newsweek down 75% in traffic) are now doing it anyway, backed by direct licensing deals with Meta and Microsoft.
- **Why us:** Sharpens the LLM-visibility conversation from "get cited" to "who has the leverage" — a harder-edged AEO angle than we've run so far this week.

**Creators are quietly becoming media conglomerates**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/creators-media-conglomerates/), Aug 6 2026
- **What:** $21bn in 2026 brand-to-creator spend (nearly double 2022); publisher-vs-creator revenue gap narrowing from 44% to ~26%. MrBeast has diversified into chocolate, toys, fintech and telecom off the back of one channel.
- **Why us:** Direct Creator Report (in progress, August) evidence that durable creator value compounds into a business, not a campaign line — "we know creators" has to mean knowing this trajectory.

**OpenAI's first influencer brand trip sparks backlash**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`

**Is your brand caught in the "AI slop"? Consumers are taking notice**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
