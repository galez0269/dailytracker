**Patreon lays off 93 people (20% of staff)**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Feed Me](https://www.readfeedme.com/p/i-dont-want-to-hide-these-clothing), Jul 27 2026
- **What:** Jack Conte's staff memo cites "rapid market changes," explicitly rules out AI as the driver, and frames the cuts as making Patreon "a stable, dependable rock" for creators — its largest reduction since 2022.

**Omnicom folds Mediahub and Hearts & Science into a single global network**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
