## Industry Buzz

**SiriusXM signs Giggly Squad (reported $160M); the deal unit is the creator ecosystem**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Giggly Squad's SiriusXM deal signals a shift from podcast networks to creator ecosystems](https://digiday.com/media/giggly-squads-siriusxm-deal-signals-a-shift-from-podcast-networks-to-creator-ecosystems/), 2 Nov 2026
- **What:** SiriusXM signed a multi-year deal (reported value $160M) with Paige DeSorbo and Hannah Berner; weeks later the pair sold out four nights at Madison Square Garden, a first for a female-hosted podcast. SiriusXM describes a move from horizontal networks to vertical ecosystems around one talent, with audio, video, social and live as "four legs of the stool", and says brands increasingly want to be across all of it. Digiday adds that Amazon's New Heights investment and Substack and beehiiv ad tools point the same way. Cited podcast data (Sounds Profitable): 84% of clip viewers say clips made them regular listeners at least sometimes; only 17% of Americans expect a podcast to be audio-only. The $160M figure is reported, not confirmed by the parties.
- **Why us:** "We know creators" now has to cover the whole creator business (audio, video, social, live, commerce), not a single post. Think Forward: a macro shift in who owns audience relationships (talent, not networks), with a clear brand implication: partner with the ecosystem and plan for the clip-to-long-form-to-live loop. Extends 08-06 "creators as media conglomerates" and the Participation arm.

**Alexa for Shopping passes 400M users while buyers start questioning Google search budgets**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday (Modern Retail) — Amazon says more than 400M customers have used Alexa for Shopping](https://digiday.com/marketing/amazon-says-more-than-400m-customers-have-used-alexa-for-shopping-in-the-last-12-months/), 2 Nov 2026; [Digiday — Google search loses its free pass with buyers](https://digiday.com/marketing/google-search-loses-its-free-pass-with-buyers/), 2 Nov 2026
- **What:** Amazon says 400M+ customers used its shopping assistant in 12 months (350M at end July) and, per CEO Andy Jassy, they spend 50% more than non-users; Amazon continues to block outside AI agents (ChatGPT, Meta's Muse) and wants Alexa to be the primary agent for shopping on Amazon. Amazon's Q3 ad growth was 11% (from 24% a year earlier), partly a Prime Day timing shift. Digiday's 18 media buyers estimate 10-30% of Google search budgets are being questioned or moved (estimates vary by agency); branded CPCs rose 5% in Q3, and most advertisers are shifting spend within Google (AI Max, Performance Max, YouTube, Demand Gen) rather than leaving it. Amazon's usage and spend figures are its own.
- **Why us:** What changed since 10-30 ("AI answers gain usage faster than they earn"): usage is now measurable and budget scrutiny has begun. Answers the LLM-visibility C-suite question with a position: when assistants are the shopper's first stop, the brand signals they draw on (earned, creator, community proof) are the asset. Think Forward: a macro shift in how people discover and choose (delegating to an assistant), with a plain brand implication for how we plan social as an input to AI answers. Extends 10-28 "be believed" and 10-30 AI Mode must-know.

**Michael Kors' TikTok Shop sells at nearly 4x plan; Gen Z "didn't know who we were"**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/michael-kors-tiktok-shop-sales-gen-z/), 30 Oct 2026 (from Capri's 30 Oct earnings call)
- **What:** Capri's CEO says TikTok Shop sales run at nearly four times initial expectations and the customer database grew 8% year on year; a $359.50 handbag driven largely by influencer interest became a top seller. Context: promo days were cut about 50% and revenue still fell 8.6% to $663M, and Capri did not say how many new contacts bought. All figures are the company's own.
- **Why us:** A heritage-luxury-adjacent brand using the shop surface as an acquisition route to a young audience, a live proof point for platform feature fluency beyond FMCG and beauty; pairs with Disney's TikTok Shop event (10-30). The honest caveat (revenue still down) is the credible way to tell it.

**AEO becomes infrastructure: People Inc builds APIs so AI agents can read its journalism; Profound makes Singapore its APAC hub**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — People Inc. is building custom APIs](https://digiday.com/media/people-inc-is-building-custom-apis-to-make-its-journalism-easier-for-ai-agents-to-use/), 2 Nov 2026; [Marketing-Interactive — Profound plants APAC flag in SG](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/ai-marketing-platform-profound-plants-apac-flag-in-sg-amid-ai-search-boom), 2 Nov 2026
- **What:** People Inc. is serving agents Markdown article text plus metadata and "trust signals" instead of full pages, citing HUMAN's report of agent and agentic-browser traffic up 7,851% year on year; Time, Forbes and The Economist are doing similar. Profound, an AI-visibility platform, is opening an APAC headquarters in Singapore, citing its own analysis of 1.8M ChatGPT and Gemini conversations (the research, comparison or purchase share up 23% from January to September).
- **Why us:** The supply side of AEO is now being built (publishers) and sold (Profound), which supports our line that what gets cited is what is credibly sourced; worth a view on what a brand's equivalent "agent-readable" proof layer is. Also a data-partner lead: see coverage notes.

- **Source:** [Glossy — Ouai launches cinematic web series](https://www.glossy.co/pop/ouai-launches-cinematic-web-series-as-long-form-content-trends-people-are-craving-a-new-version-of-authenticity/), 30 Oct 2026; [Adweek — Svedka hands its next Super Bowl ad to the public after polarizing AI debut](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/svedka-hands-its-next-super-bowl-ad-to-the-public-after-polarizing-ai-debut/), 2 Nov 2026 (date from Adweek feed); [Adweek — e.l.f. - **What:** P&G's Ouai spent nearly a year on "Chopped", a three-part slasher-comedy web series (Victoria Pedretti), and plans to try Instagram's 90-minute Features; its VP says "Trust comes from humans" and describes a swing from lo-fi back to production value, citing Prada's hand-drawn film. Svedka, after a polarising AI-made 2026 Super Bowl debut, is giving a winner $500K to make its next spot (Andy Cohen fronts the contest). e.l.f.'s first holiday ad is stop-motion and claymation with Alan Cumming narrating. None of the three reports performance data, and Adweek's pages carry no visible dates.
- **Why us:** Think Forward: the pattern across brands and categories is effort used as proof (the 10-26 Zoe Scaman "Tithe" argument and the 10-30 Prada and Firefox item, now with a fourth and fifth brand). Brand implication: use AI behind the work, make human craft or fan participation the visible part, and let the format (long-form, handmade, crowdsourced) carry the signal. Unfakeable capital in practice.
