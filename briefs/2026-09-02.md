## Industry Buzz

**Meta's PR counter-offensive against TikTok and YouTube**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/social-marketing/metas-push-on-rivals-may-be-pr-genius-if-it-doesnt-overplay-its-hand/), Sep 1 2026
- **What:** Days after its up-to-$18bn youth-safety settlement, Meta launched newspaper ads pressuring TikTok and YouTube to adopt the same teen-safety measures it's now required to implement — a crisis-comms move experts call savvy but risky if it overplays its hand.
- **Why us:** A live case study in turning a forced compliance cost into a competitive offensive — worth watching for what credible corporate crisis response looks like versus what reads as opportunistic, right as yesterday's settlement is still fresh.

**WARC/Plaid: Gen Z's AI-trust paradox**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/half-of-gen-z-and-millennials-prefer-sharing-financial-details-with-ai-over-humans-918879244aa84655a7e0e8b8a7259d06) (citing Plaid/Harris Poll), Aug 31 2026
- **What:** 52% of US Gen Z/millennials are more comfortable sharing financial information with AI than a human advisor, and 88% think AI will improve digital commerce — yet 53% of Gen Z say they avoid brands that use AI in marketing or advertising.
- **Why us:** A precise, quotable answer to "does Gen Z trust AI" — the honest answer is "it depends where," and that nuance is exactly the sharper framing a C-suite AI conversation needs instead of a blanket yes or no.

**WARC: Gen Z's news-trust paradox**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/brands-need-to-be-aware-of-a-gen-z-trust-paradox-1f25c90d99064f3699416bde755305d1), Sep 1 2026
- **What:** 62% of Gen Z get their news from social platforms — the top source — yet only 33% trust those platforms, a gap WARC argues brands need to account for in how they communicate.
- **Why us:** Corroborates the AI-trust paradox above with a second, independent trust gap — reach and credibility aren't the same currency, and platform presence alone won't earn trust.
