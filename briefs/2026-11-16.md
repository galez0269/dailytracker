## Industry Buzz

**Unilever's social-first model: 500K creators, half of digital budget to social, reorganisation in 2027**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — How Unilever's social-first marketing is evolving amid reorganization](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/how-unilevers-social-first-marketing-is-evolving-amid-reorganization/874457/), 12 Nov 2026
- **What:** Unilever's model covers four areas (recommendation, content, influence, media). It aims to move half its digital ad budget into social and creator-led work and now works with 500,000 creators. It reports 12x more content than in 2024 at 90% lower cost and creator content at 1.5x the ROI of brand-led content, and says video loses value after about four days. A new operating model with six global units ("global strategy, local execution") starts in 2027, and Leandro Barreto becomes chief growth and marketing officer on 1 Jan. All figures are company-reported, from its 4 Nov investor event.
- **Why us:** This sets the benchmark FMCG clients will be held to. Our angle is the gap in the numbers: video that loses value in four days is volume, not compounding. We can show how to make creator work at scale add up over time (repeat creators, community, distinctive assets), so it builds Proof and Power rather than refilling a feed. Read alongside Guinness's go-slow approach (POVs).

**Publishers sell AI visibility: BDG and Profound get Pampers and Innisfree cited in AI answers**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — How Bustle Digital Group is helping Pampers and Innisfree show up in AI recommendations](https://digiday.com/media/how-bustle-digital-group-is-helping-pampers-and-innisfree-show-up-in-ai-recommendations/), 16 Nov 2026; context: [Digiday — Yahoo becomes first licensee of 'Spotify-like' collective AI content licensing model](https://digiday.com/media/yahoo-becomes-first-licensee-of-spotify-like-collective-ai-content-licensing-model/), 12 Nov 2026; [Digiday — IAB finds AI measurement standards elusive amid platform data gaps](https://digiday.com/media/media-briefing-the-iab-finds-ai-measurement-standards-elusive-for-publishers-amid-platform-data-gaps/), 12 Nov 2026
- **What:** BDG's offering (called ECHO internally) uses AI agents trained on its content to write briefs; its editors turn them into articles meant to be cited by AI assistants, and Profound measures the result. At least three brands have paid. Innisfree's visibility across 200 skincare prompts rose 30%, and Pampers moved from #2 to #1 for fragrance-free diaper questions (BDG/Profound figures, no revenue disclosed). In the same week Yahoo became the first licensee of a collective AI-licensing scheme for publishers, and the IAB's new AI attribution framework is limited by platforms not sharing data.
- **Why us:** AEO is turning into a media buy. Our position: buying citations is one route; the other is being the brand that communities, creators and reviewers already talk about, which the AI then repeats. That is Proof built over time, and we are better placed than a publisher to build it. Follows the 11-05 and 11-06 AEO must-knows; the uplift figures come from the people selling the product.

**AI in the workflow has a trust gap: 80% of marketers see inaccurate AI findings, 36% check most of the time**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — AI-derived audience insights hurt results: What the numbers say](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/ai-derived-audience-insights-hurt-results-what-the-numbers-say/874565/), 13 Nov 2026; [Marketing Dive — Sociable: Meta's AI ads included unapproved discounts](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/sociable-metas-ai-ads-included-unapproved-discounts/874525/), 12 Nov 2026
- **What:** GWI surveyed 1,883 senior insights, agency and marketing professionals (UK, US, Singapore, Q3 2026). 80% often get inaccurate or misleading AI findings, only 36% verify outputs most of the time, nearly 70% have seen AI misrepresent their target audience, and 82% report at least one bad outcome (financial loss 27%, reputational damage 26%). Separately, Meta's AI ad tools reportedly pulled discounts from a store's website into Facebook and Instagram ads with promotions switched off (found by an advisor, first reported by PPC Land; no Meta response in the piece).
- **Why us:** This is the counterweight to the "AI across the value chain" push: AI makes the work faster, but someone has to check what it says about real people. That is our cultural-insights team's job, and we can say it with GWI's numbers. GWI is a vendor with an interest in the finding; still useful as a data point. Follows the 11-05 brief-quality must-know.

- **Source:** [Digiday — 'We're not trying to be TwitchTok': Twitch adds new short-form video feature](https://digiday.com/media/were-not-trying-to-be-twitchtok-twitch-adds-new-short-form-video-feature/), 13 Nov 2026; [Marketing Dive — Twitch debuts shoppable, skippable ads as TwitchCon feels growing pains](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/twitch-debuts-shoppable-skippable-ads-as-twitchcon-feels-growing-pains/874614/), 13 Nov 2026 (standfirst only); context: [BoF podcast — Is Fashion Sleeping on Twitch and Discord?](https://www.businessoffashion.com/podcasts/technology/is-fashion-sleeping-on-twitch-and-discord/), 11 Nov 2026

## Trends

**Craftwashing: effort claims get checked, and the anger is about deception**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Was Dior's hand-drawn holiday campaign a case of AI-powered 'craftwashing'?](https://www.glossy.co/podcasts/glossy-podcast-was-diors-hand-drawn-holiday-campaign-a-case-of-ai-powered-craftwashing/), 13 Nov 2026; [WHAT'S ANU — Dior Drama + Signal Scan](https://whatsanu.substack.com/p/dior-drama-signal-scan-11926), 9 Nov 2026 (paid, intro only); [BoF — In Fashion's Holiday Campaigns, the Medium Is the Message](https://www.businessoffashion.com/articles/marketing-pr/fashion-holiday-campaigns-medium-is-the-message/), 12 Nov 2026 (paywalled); [Link in Bio — Context is king](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/context-is-king), 12 Nov 2026 (paid, preview only)
- **What:** Dior's film, made with animator Sylvain Chomet, was promoted as fully hand-drawn. Chomet and the producer told Variety (via Glossy) they did not use AI to generate it but used "automated interpolation" for some in-between frames. ANU argues people are angry about being misled, not about the tool, and calls the wider pattern "humanwashing" (for example, vintage tees artificially faded). BoF says handmade holiday work (Prada, Rimowa) now needs more openness about process. Rachel Karten reports that behind-the-scenes "proof of reality" posts are beating the campaigns they support.
- **Why us:** New since the 11-05 and 11-06 handmade items: the claim itself is now the risk. Our line for clients: show how it was made, say exactly where tools were used, and treat the making-of as the main content. That is unfakeable capital in practice and a Think Forward behaviour shift (audiences checking whether things are real). Dior has not responded directly in anything we saw.

- **What:** BoF Insights and Amazon Fashion & Sports surveyed 10,000 consumers and 150 senior leaders in the UK, Germany, France, Italy and Spain. - **Why us:** Think Forward material: trust moving from big voices to smaller, closer ones. Fits the 11-06 Reddit fashion-index item (proof moving to the crowd) and argues for creator programmes built on many trusted smaller voices. BoF Insights is a possible data partner; Amazon co-authored, so check for bias.

**Maybelline bans the word "sponsorship"**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Inside Maybelline's third summer in the 'Love Island' villa](https://www.glossy.co/beauty/maybelline-love-island-usa-lifter-gel/), 11 Nov 2026 — Yasmin Dastmalchi, Maybelline and Essie USA

- **Why us:** Agree and extend: a beauty brand showing in numbers that being part of the fan experience beats logo placement. A clean Participation case for entertainment partnerships; third year running, so it is compounding too.
