## Industry Buzz

**Creator marketing hits $116B; paid amplification and creator-made ads drive the growth**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Brands booking creators to make their ads are pushing up $116B global influencer market](https://digiday.com/media-buying/brands-booking-creators-to-make-their-ads-are-pushing-up-116b-global-influencer-market/), 18 Nov 2026; [Marketing Dive — Paid media now plays largest role in brand-funded creator growth: report](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/paid-media-now-plays-largest-role-in-brand-funded-creator-growth-report/875032/), 18 Nov 2026; [Digiday — Why more brands are paying creators to make their ads](https://digiday.com/marketing/why-more-brands-are-paying-creators-to-make-their-ads/), 20 Nov 2026
- **What:** Madison & Wall puts brand-funded creator spend at $116B in 2026 (up 15%), heading to $178B by 2030, about twice the growth of the total ad market. Paid amplification of creator content is 44% (~$52B), ahead of sponsored posts and ambassador deals at 28%; creators making ad assets for paid social, CTV and display is 12% but growing fastest (~16% a year). The growth comes mostly from budgets moved out of traditional media, search, paid social and programmatic. Agencies report client creator spend up 20–50% this year; Digiday cites a System1/WPP Media/Goat study finding creator ads gave category leaders 3.2x the awareness lift of traditional creative, and creator fees up 10–20% as brands demand more usage rights.
- **Why us:** Creator work has moved from the influencer budget to the media plan. Our angle: we know creators and we know paid social, so we can say which creators to cast, which content to put paid behind, and how to buy the rights. Pairs with the 11-17 taxonomy item (you can't price what you can't classify). Madison & Wall is an analyst firm; Digiday's two pieces use $116B and $112B for the base.

**ChatGPT Business Hub: first-party AI visibility data, and a legal-terms scare**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Media Briefing: ChatGPT's new business hub is an early 'search console moment' for publishers](https://digiday.com/media/media-briefing-chatgpts-new-business-hub-is-an-early-search-console-moment-for-publishers/), 19 Nov 2026; [Digiday — OpenAI rewrites ChatGPT Business Hub terms after legal language raises concerns](https://digiday.com/media/openai-rewrites-chatgpt-business-hub-terms-after-legal-language-raises-concerns/), 20 Nov 2026
- **What:** OpenAI launched a Business Hub giving verified businesses data on ChatGPT mentions, profile views, website clicks and the topics that lead people to them. Half of six large publishers Digiday spoke to were signing up; critics note there is no prompt-level data and it only tracks a business's own site. The business terms (published 10 Nov) included a release of legal claims, an arbitration clause and a class-action waiver that lawyers said could cover content published before sign-up; after Digiday asked on 18 Nov, OpenAI narrowed the release to claims about a business's own Business Agent.
- **Why us:** AEO (a C-suite hot button) now has a platform-native measure, like Google's Search Console. This follows the 11-16 BDG/Profound and 11-17 Glossy GEO items: we can now show clients which conversations bring ChatGPT to them, then use community and creator content to shape those conversations. Practical note for account teams: have legal read the terms before signing clients up.

**Coca-Cola drops generative AI from its holiday ads**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — Coca-Cola pivots from divisive AI-generated ads for holiday campaign](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/coca-cola-pivots-from-divisive-ai-generated-ads-for-holiday-campaign/875215/), 19 Nov 2026
- **What:** Coca-Cola's "Enjoy Joy" holiday campaign (WPP Open X, led by Ogilvy) shows family and friends in seasonal moments across TV, digital, creators, packaging and Amazon media, and drops the generative AI used in the previous two holiday campaigns. Marketing Dive says those AI ads divided consumers even though they beat internal benchmarks.
- **Why us:** Counterweight to "AI across the value chain": the most-watched AI ad has backed off. With Dior's craftwashing story (11-16), our line is simple: use AI in how we make the work, but people need to feel that real people made it. Unfakeable capital; worth a short POV before clients plan 2027 holiday work.

**Advertisers pause TikTok in Spain after a child-abuse content investigation**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Advertisers suspend TikTok activity in response to Spanish CSAM investigation](https://digiday.com/media-buying/advertisers-suspend-tiktok-activity-in-response-to-spanish-csam-investigation/), 19 Nov 2026
- **What:** A Maldita.es investigation found child sexual abuse material, or ads for sites selling it, running next to ads from 21 brands including Ralph Lauren, Pringles, Samsung, Apple and JD Sports. Banco Santander and Moeve paused TikTok; Mediaplus Equmedia says three clients paused and expect to stay off into 2027. Spain's youth minister asked the data protection agency to suspend TikTok temporarily, and prosecutors are investigating. TikTok says the material is banned and reported.
- **Why us:** A brand-safety question every client with Spanish or EU spend will ask this week. Our answer should cover both paid placement controls and the creator and community side, where risk is lower. Platform governance thread; watch for spread beyond Spain.

**Instagram lets creators add Amazon affiliate links to Reels**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`

**Gen Z leans on AI for company, and pushes back with "dumb" tech**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [After School — Bot Besties and Brain Gains](https://afterschool.substack.com/p/bot-besties-and-brain-gains), 18 Nov 2026 (summarising Gallup, Semafor and NPR); [Dazed — How the Nintendo DS became 2026's hottest instrument](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/71376/1/ds-old-school-games-consoles-are-the-instruments-of-the-future-nintendo), 18 Nov 2026

For brands, the question is whether to be a helpful companion or an escape. Survey figures are second-hand via After School; check the originals before citing.

## POVs

**Eugene Healey: the next "cool girl" can't be summarised**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Considered Chaos — What's the next cool girl archetype?](https://eugenehealey.substack.com/p/dua-lipa-cool-girl-archetype), 18 Nov 2026 — Eugene Healey
- **What:** Healey argues "cool" is shaped by the media of the time: from Kate Moss (magazines) to Emma Chamberlain (social intimacy) to Dua Lipa (breadth across feeds). He says the breadth model is now overdone and proposes three successors: the Obsessive (depth), the Ghost (influence through other people) and the Host (experiences). If AI chatbots become the next medium, he says, cool will be whatever can't be summarised.
- **Why us:** Agree and extend. It links AEO and unfakeable capital: the more AI summarises everything, the more valuable depth and real-world presence become. Useful for creator casting (choose the Obsessive over the generalist) and a strong Think Forward framing.

**Melanie Bender (Lore, ex-Rhode): "world-building" has to start from beliefs**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio — Has 'world-building' lost its meaning?](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/lore-world-building-strategy), 19 Nov 2026 — Rachel Karten with Melanie Bender
- **What:** Karten says "world-building" has become a buzzword like "authenticity". Bender says a real brand world starts from beliefs, then shapes colour, references and product; each Lore fragrance is built around a feeling, and one launch included an original cartoon series and a popsicle collaboration. Lore doesn't generate images or video with AI. Bender names erl, Jacquemus and Starface as brands doing it well.
- **Why us:** Agree. It's the Loop's Presence and Power in practice: consistent beliefs that show up everywhere, not just mood boards. A useful test for client "brand world" briefs, and a no-generative-AI stance that fits today's Coca-Cola must-know.
