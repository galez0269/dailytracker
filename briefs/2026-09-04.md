## Industry Buzz

**PepsiCo moves its global media account to Publicis, ending decades with Omnicom**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Ad Age](https://adage.com/agencies/accounts-in-review/aa-pepsico-shifts-global-media-to-publicis-from-omnicom/), Sep 2026; also [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/pepsico-picks-new-global-media-agency), Sep 2026
- **What:** PepsiCo moved its ~$1.9bn global media account to Publicis without running a formal pitch, ending a multi-decade relationship with Omnicom/OMD; Publicis is to build an AI- and data-driven "One PepsiCo" model spanning strategy, planning, activation and connected identity across 200+ markets.
- **Why us:** Direct AI-in-value-chain C-suite hit — a marquee advertiser is choosing a network on its AI operating model, not just its media plan; sharpens our own "AI priced on outcome" pitch against holdco-scale AI promises. FMCG Report material.

**A DTC wellness brand let AI agents plan and buy its TV ads — and made 5x its money back**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/wellness-brand-rouge-care-let-ai-agents-buy-its-tv-ads-and-made-5x-its-money-back/), Sep 2026
- **What:** Wellness brand Rouge Care ran two AI agents — one for planning, one for audience discovery/buying — via PubMatic's AgenticOS for a CTV campaign; $25K in spend drove $125K in attributable sales, doubling its ROAS target.
- **Why us:** A hard, sourced number for "AI embedded across the value chain" — exactly the proof point the C-suite asks for beyond promise. Beauty & Wellness category.
