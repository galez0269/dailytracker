## Industry Buzz

**Amazon's DSP becomes a ChatGPT ad-buying channel**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Amazon brings its DSP to OpenAI's ChatGPT ads, extending its supply-chasing streak](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazon-brings-its-dsp-to-openais-chatgpt-ads-extending-its-supply-chasing-streak/), Sept 10 2026
- **What:** Select US advertisers can now buy ChatGPT ad inventory (CPC or CPM, text/image units under organic responses) through Amazon's DSP; Delta Vacations is the pilot's first advertiser, and Amazon undercuts rivals with near-zero DSP fees.
- **Why us:** AEO stops being a framework question and becomes a live media buy with real infrastructure behind it — worth having a point of view on whether clients should be testing this now or waiting for it to mature.

**Wikipedia fights the "answer engine apocalypse"**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Inside Wikipedia's Plan to Survive the Answer Engine Apocalypse](https://www.adweek.com/media/wikipedia-survive-answer-engine-apocalypse/), Sept 16 2026
- **What:** Wikipedia's human traffic is down ~8% year-over-year as AI answer engines cite its content without sending visitors back; its response combines a 25th-anniversary brand campaign, a paid enterprise API for LLM companies, and a renewed push on individual contributors over institutional branding.
- **Why us:** The starkest version yet of the AEO paradox — "more visible than ever, and yet less known" is a line any brand relying on AI citation for reach should sit with.

**Brands reinvest in physical retail as AI erodes digital acquisition**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Brands refocus on physical retail as AI disrupts digital customer acquisition](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/brands-refocus-on-physical-retail-as-ai-disrupts-digital-customer-acquisition/), Sept 16 2026
- **What:** Fashion and beauty founders describe search and display ads as "very difficult to advertise on" now that AI chat mediates product discovery, and are shifting budget into in-store parties, events and personal service instead — "our customer is looking for a human. They want to touch, they want to feel."
- **Why us:** A concrete, capital-allocation-level proof point for Presence-via-Participation — AI disruption is pushing budget toward exactly the kind of physical, relationship-led activation this network already does well.

**The PepsiCo/Coke media fallout keeps compounding**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`

- **Why us:** Extends the 09-09 Coca-Cola/Publicis must-know with the holdco's own reaction on the record; sharpens the compounding-not-consolidating pitch against a specific, named competitive loss.

**Google's ad-tech antitrust remedies land as behavioural, not structural**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Google's ad tech remedies decoded: what changes, what doesn't and what will take years](https://digiday.com/media-buying/googles-ad-tech-remedies-decoded-what-changes-what-doesnt-and-what-will-take-years/), Sept 16 2026

**Unilever tries to close the "memory gap" in consumer research**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
