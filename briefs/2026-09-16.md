## Industry Buzz

**AI-search visibility has no universal playbook**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — In Graphic Detail: Inside the scramble to measure a brand's AI visibility](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-inside-the-scramble-to-measure-a-brands-ai-visibility/), Sept 15 2026
- **What:** Different AI assistants pull from entirely different source pools — ChatGPT favours Reddit, Gemini favours YouTube — so a brand can be visible on one engine and invisible on another; IAB is drafting standardised measurement frameworks because 45% of marketers say measurement is their top AI-visibility hurdle.
- **Why us:** Direct hit on the LLM-visibility C-suite question — the honest answer this week is "there's no single AEO score," which is itself a useful, differentiated thing to be able to say.

**Mastercard replaces its 14-year comms agency, citing AI**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Interactive — Mastercard appoints new global comms agency partner](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mastercard-appoints-new-global-comms-agency-partner), Sept 16 2026
- **What:** After a six-month review, Mastercard ends its ~14-year Ketchum relationship for We Communications, wanting a partner who can carry its narrative into AI, agentic commerce and cybersecurity — beyond "Priceless."
- **Why us:** A marquee global brand explicitly re-scoping its comms remit around AI capability, not creative execution — a live instance of what "embedding AI across the value chain" looks like from the client side, and a fresh incumbency-loss story for the agency-landscape thread.

**Boots proves AI creative-scoring moves real media KPIs**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [WARC — How Boots is operationalising creative effectiveness](https://www.warc.com/article/how-boots-is-operationalizing-creative-effectiveness-408d88c60bcd46a69b06145235e1eec9), Sept 15 2026
- **What:** Boots partnered with CreativeX and DAIVID to score ~3,500 creative assets for quality, attention and emotion, then used the scores to improve Meta/YouTube view-through rates and cut cost-per-completed-view.
- **Why us:** A concrete, numbers-backed AI-in-value-chain proof point — "the gap between creative and media is closing" is exactly the pitch for AI priced on outcome, not novelty.
