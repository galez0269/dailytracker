## Industry Buzz

**Meta bans TikTok-linked ads across its apps**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Meta's TikTok Link Ban Could Hobble Media Buyers' Creator Campaigns](https://www.adweek.com/social-marketing/meta-tiktok-ad-ban-advertisers/), Oct 13 2026 (also [Business of Fashion — Meta Bans Ads From TikTok on Its Apps](https://www.businessoffashion.com/news/marketing-pr/meta-bans-ads-from-tiktok-on-its-apps/), Oct 9). Adweek piece is paywalled, intro only.
- **What:** Enforcement began Oct 8 in the U.S., Canada, Japan, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam and Egypt, per Bloomberg. Meta won't run ads from TikTok, and it also rejects ads from other advertisers that link to TikTok or other ByteDance properties. Meta calls it ordinary business practice. Media buyers told Adweek that blocking a rival makes sense, but blocking brands from linking to TikTok is a different matter.
- **Why us:** The platforms are walling themselves off, so a creator programme can no longer assume paid traffic moves freely between them. This is platform feature fluency in its most practical form: the plan has to be built natively for each platform. It's a good moment to tell clients what to change in creator amplification this quarter.

**AI visibility moves from the search team to the brand team**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Ikea's CMO sees LLMs as the newest place a brand has to hold its line](https://digiday.com/marketing/ikeas-cmo-sees-llms-as-the-newest-place-a-brand-has-to-hold-its-line/), Oct 14 2026; [Digiday — Brands turn to content series and Jimi Hendrix to improve AI search performance](https://digiday.com/marketing/brands-turn-to-content-series-and-jimi-hendrix-to-improve-ai-search-performance/), Oct 15; [BoF Insights — How Fashion Brands Get AI Visibility Right](https://www.businessoffashion.com/reports/technology/bof-insights-brand-pulse-report-how-fashion-brands-get-ai-visibility-right/), Oct 13 (summary only); [Glossy+ Research — How agencies adapt to a media landscape shaped by AI and creators](https://www.glossy.co/research/glossy-research-how-agencies-adapt-to-a-media-landscape-shaped-by-ai-and-creators/), Oct 13
- **What:** Ikea's Vincenzo Riili says LLMs draw on everything written about a brand, so brands that keep changing their positioning for short-term sales confuse the models. In his view AI should distribute the brand, not create it. A Dentsu Creative survey of 1,950 senior marketers found 81% expect AI agents to make brand preference more important, while 78% fear agents will make the brand experience invisible. Fender rebuilt its site around heritage and artist stories, and Marshall is running "Dead Venues", a YouTube series staging new bands in closed venues. Both say better AI-search visibility is part of the aim. BoF's analysis of 120 fashion brands found that being understood by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini doesn't guarantee being recommended. Glossy's survey of 30 agency staff found 77% plan to raise AI-search/GEO budgets within a year.
- **Why us:** This is the strongest week yet for our AEO argument. Consistency and distinctive cultural content are what models learn from, which is the compounding thesis applied to LLMs. Consistent Proof earns the citation, and a "brand brain" can't be built from campaigns that change every quarter. It's also a direct answer to the C-suite question on LLM visibility.

**Publicis goes over the CMO's head**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Publicis is selling directly to AI-anxious CEOs as it steps away from traditional pitches](https://digiday.com/media-buying/publicis-is-selling-directly-to-ai-anxious-ceos-as-it-steps-away-from-traditional-pitches/), Oct 13 2026
- **What:** Q3 net revenue rose 5% to €3.53B. Sadoun says new clients add about 2 points of organic growth heading into 2027. PepsiCo's global media account moved to Publicis without a pitch, and Publicis dropped out of the Coca-Cola media pitch. Sadoun credits Sapient with winning "the two biggest accounts of the year without a pitch".
- **Why us:** Holdcos are selling AI transformation in the boardroom, which squeezes the CMO. Last week's Lippincott study made the same point. Our counter-position is cultural relevance as a growth lever that CEOs also care about, and it gives CMOs something to bring upstairs. The Think Forward framing should speak to the CEO as well as the CMO.

**The creator economy splits: creators as partners, or creators as media**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Media buys and mega partnerships: How the creator economy is splitting in two](https://digiday.com/podcasts/media-buys-and-mega-partnerships-how-the-creator-economy-is-splitting-in-two/), Oct 13 2026; [Digiday — Future of TV Briefing: TikTok Shop success = small creators + more videos](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/future-of-tv-briefing-tiktok-shop-success-small-creators-more-videos/), Oct 14
- **What:** Top creators such as MrBeast and Alix Earle now work like agencies on long-term brand deals, while nano and micro creators are bought programmatically at scale. Flywheel's analysis of 1.5M TikTok Shop samples sent to creators found only 47% of creators made a video and 14% drove a sale. Smaller creators drive more sales per 1,000 followers, and creators posting 20+ videos average $74 per video against $7 for a single post.
- **Why us:** It's a useful split for the Creator Report: creator as creative partner (our wheelhouse) and creator as media (a buying problem). The data backs consistency over one-off posts, which is compounding at the level of the individual creator. We can advise on both models and on when to use each.

- **Source:** [Digiday — Inside Dentsu's plan to make entertainment a part of every brand's budget](https://digiday.com/marketing/inside-dentsus-plan-to-make-entertainment-a-part-of-every-brands-budget/), Oct 15 2026

**Instagram tests recommending Reels over three minutes to non-followers**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio — Is your social strategy memorable?](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/good-marketing-plays-the-long-game), Oct 8 2026 — Rachel Karten (exclusive)

**The "IRL influencer": community building becomes a paid creator job**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [After School — Bumble Fumble and Stream Rot](https://afterschool.substack.com/p/bumble-fumble-and-stream-rot), Oct 14 2026 — Casey Lewis (summarising The Atlantic's [The Rise of the IRL Influencer](https://www.theatlantic.com/health/2026/10/community-builders/688947/), Oct 13, plus BBC and Vanity Fair); corroborated by [Glossy — How to use IRL events to build community against a 'sea of sameness' online](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/how-to-use-irl-events-to-build-community-against-a-sea-of-sameness-online/), Oct 12
- **What:** A new class of "community builders" is making money from Gen Z's appetite for IRL connection (knitting circles, silent reading parties, rooftop singles nights), often with brand sponsors. The platform Pie pays hosts up to $3,500 a month to run gatherings. In the same issue, Bumble is being taken private for $1.3B as young people delete dating apps, and Tinder has launched Events in 20 cities. Glossy reports brands moving from creator-only events to open, neighbourhood-scale meetups.
- **Why us:** Building communities is our wheelhouse, and the host has become the creator. It's a Participation arm that brands can sponsor without owning, and it's the next chapter of the analogue thread that has now run for months. Creator Report and Think Forward material. The question to answer for clients is how to fund a host without turning the gathering into an ad.

## POVs

**Eugene Healey: images are losing their cultural power**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Considered Chaos — Images are losing their cultural power](https://eugenehealey.substack.com/p/images-are-losing-their-power), Oct 14 2026 — Eugene Healey
- **What:** Healey argues that abundant images and algorithmic distribution have shrunk the gap between discovery and mass spread, which is where "cool" used to live, until coolness can't really exist. Scarce objects like the Birkin end up culturally everywhere, aesthetics get copied without anyone living them, and fashion becomes LARP: signs that no longer point to anything.
- **Why us:** We agree, and it gives us a sharp way to say "unfakeable capital". When the image costs nothing, value moves to what it costs to actually live the culture: time, risk, community. That's Proof over appearance, and it supports this week's IRL and long-form items. Think Forward material, and a strong frame for luxury (Power).
