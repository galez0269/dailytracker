## Industry Buzz

**OpenAI's ChatGPT ads go conversational**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — OpenAI's next ChatGPT ad format: click to chat, not to site](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-next-chatgpt-ad-format-click-to-chat-not-to-site/), Sept 14 2026
- **What:** A new ChatGPT ad format keeps the interaction inside the chat window — users click "Chat with us" and talk to the brand's own AI agent rather than leaving for a website. Wayfair is piloting it "at limited scale with guardrails."
- **Why us:** AEO now has a literal media buy attached to it — clients asking about LLM visibility will next ask whether they should be building a conversational ad presence, not just optimising for citation.

**Brand purchase consideration falls to a 5-year low**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Ad Age — Brand purchase consideration at 5-year low: Morning Consult](https://adage.com/brand-marketing/aa-purchase-consideration-5-year-low-morning-consult-survey/), Sept 9 2026
- **What:** Only 14% of tracked brands grew purchase consideration in 2026, versus 94% a year ago; trust, usage and favourability are cooling in parallel.
- **Why us:** Hard, industry-wide evidence for the "don't hoard cultural capital, compound it" thesis — consideration is collapsing exactly where brands have been spiking rather than compounding. Q2 Digital Culture Review and Think Forward material.

**AI reshapes agency economics, but contracts are still catching up**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — AI reshapes agency economics, but their contracts are still scrambling to catch up](https://digiday.com/marketing/ai-reshapes-agency-economics-but-their-contracts-are-still-scrambling-to-catch-up/), Sept 10 2026
- **What:** Agencies are shifting toward SaaS-style, outcome-linked billing under generative/agentic AI, but for now are bolting riders onto existing contracts (data use, human oversight, IP, AI-tool disclosure) rather than rewriting them.
- **Why us:** Direct hit on the AI-in-value-chain C-suite question; sharpens an "AI priced on outcome, not token count" pitch — extends the 07-31 "second bill" thread.

**Anthropic builds out an APAC marketing/comms function**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Interactive — Anthropic names ANZ comms lead as APAC expansion gathers pace](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/anthropic-names-anz-comms-lead-as-apac-expansion-gathers-pace), Sept 9 2026
- **What:** Anthropic hires an ANZ communications lead, is adding nine more Sydney roles and a founding APAC field-marketing leader in Singapore.
- **Why us:** The AI labs themselves are becoming conventional marketing organisations and regional advertisers, not just infrastructure vendors — a live instance of "AI across the value chain" from the vendor side, and a new category of client competing for the same regional talent and agency attention as everyone else.
