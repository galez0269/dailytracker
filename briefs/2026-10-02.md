## Industry Buzz

**OpenAI's measurement gaps keep ChatGPT Ads budgets at test level**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — OpenAI's measurement gaps are keeping ChatGPT ads budgets at test level](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-measurement-gaps-are-keeping-chatgpt-ads-budgets-at-test-level/), Oct 1 2026
- **What:** Seven months in, Accuracast saw 100 reported clicks against 20 tracked, and zero conversions logged despite confirmed form fills. Conversion data lags 24–36 hours (Google takes a few hours), and clients are holding at £10–20K test budgets.
- **Why us:** This is the counterweight to Monday's $1B run-rate story. A useful line for any C-suite AEO conversation: "show up in the answer before you pay to sit beside it."

**Agentic shopping: who gets recommended**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — In Graphic Detail: Where brands stand when AI agents do the shopping](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-where-brands-stand-when-ai-agents-do-the-shopping/), Oct 1 2026
- **What:** Each engine concentrates recommendations differently. Copilot cited top-100 retailers in 34% of answers, more than three times Google AI Mode's rate, and Alexa's toy answers drew on 177 brands almost entirely from Amazon's own store. A year ago non-AI traffic was worth 128% more per visit; now AI-referred visits are worth 53% more. Human Security says 84% of Muse agent requests hit product or search pages.
- **Why us:** Hard data for the AEO hot button. Each engine has its own "shelf", so visibility has to be built per engine, much as social strategy is built per platform. That is platform-feature fluency applied to answer engines, and it fits the agent-economy thread (Dots, Muse, Scaman's "Identity Shift").
