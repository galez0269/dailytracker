## Industry Buzz

**Amazon puts ads inside its texting shopping agent; Reddit tests ads in search**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Amazon's shopping agent now takes orders by text, with Sponsored Product ads in the replies](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazons-shopping-agent-now-takes-orders-by-text-with-sponsored-product-ads-in-the-replies/), 6 Nov 2026; companion: [Adweek — Amazon is Letting Customers Text Its Shopping Assistant](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/amazon-is-letting-customers-text-its-shopping-assistant/), 6 Nov 2026 (standfirst only); [Marketing Dive — Sociable: Reddit tests ads in search results](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/sociable-reddit-tests-ads-in-search-results/873874/), 5 Nov 2026
- **What:** Alexa for Shopping now works over text message (invite-only US group, opening to "millions" in coming weeks): people message it like a friend, send screenshots for lookalikes, set price alerts, or switch on auto-buy. Sponsored Products can appear among the options it texts back, free to advertisers for now; purchases the agent completes on its own stay ad-free. Amazon says 400M+ customers used Alexa for Shopping in 12 months and agentic-feature use is up 8x since January (company figures). Separately, Reddit is testing query-targeted ads in in-app search with select advertisers, citing its own study that 72% of users come for information; this follows the 11-03 Q3 report of 1M+ lost weekly referrals. New since the 11-02 Alexa item and 11-05 ChatGPT-ads must-know: the agents themselves are now selling placement.
- **Why us:** The LLM-visibility story now has two routes into the shortlist: pay for the slot, or be the product the agent already rates. A paid slot is small and only shows at the choosing stage; the agent's default choice is shaped by reviews, community talk and creator content, which is Proof built over time. Tell clients to fund both, and to treat the organic route as brand-building, not a performance line.

**Creators had the year, Christmas still went to celebrities**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Future of Marketing Briefing: Creators had a big year. Christmas still went to the celebrities](https://digiday.com/marketing/future-of-marketing-briefing-creators-had-a-big-year-christmas-still-went-to-the-celebrities/), 6 Nov 2026; corroboration: [Adweek — Ads of the Week](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/ads-of-the-week-11-campaigns-that-caught-our-eye-from-calvin-klein-to-dunkin/), 6 Nov 2026 (Ulta with Selena Gomez and Tate McRae, Dunkin' with Channing Tatum, Calvin Klein with Jude Bellingham)
- **What:** Sephora (Seth Rogen), Kay (Colin Jost), Sports Direct (Nike athletes), Gucci (Mariah Carey) and Tesco lead with celebrities. A Kantar creative lead says brands are "borrowing fame" for a mass moment that needs a face every generation recognises, and the Christmas ad is the most scrutinised, most senior sign-off of the year. Counter-cases: Anthropologie gave its holiday campaign to comedian-creator Pooja Tripathi (script to shoot), with about 50 more creators spreading it; Marc Jacobs bills a social personality alongside celebrities; SharkNinja calls the celebrity-creator line "super fluid"; Lego hired a WME dealmaker as head of celebrity talent and brand-first partnerships.
- **Why us:** We know creators, so we should own the honest answer: creators rarely have the cross-generational recognition a Christmas spot needs, but they own the weeks around it, where the work gets seen and talked about. The pitch is "celebrity for the moment, creators for the run-up and the follow-through", which is the compounding argument applied to the year's biggest spend. Anthropologie is the proof case to watch.

**Instagram's 90-minute "Features" push: creators say exposure isn't payment**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Creators see promise in Instagram's long-form push, but question whether exposure is enough](https://digiday.com/media/creators-see-promise-in-instagrams-long-form-push-but-question-whether-exposure-is-enough/), 6 Nov 2026
- **What:** Instagram is paying a handful of creators $50K-$500K to post long-form "Features" but says it is "too early" for a long-form monetisation model. Of 50 Shine Talent Group creators surveyed, 93% said revenue policies would have to change before they post long-form there (all 50 already post on Instagram). Managers expect creators to recycle older YouTube catalogue onto Features first; some see interview shows and scripted comedy suiting the Instagram TV app's scroll-led discovery; Open Influence says the competition gives talent leverage on exclusivity and creative control. New since the 10-28 Features item and 11-05 serialised-shows item: creator pushback on the economics.
- **Why us:** Platform-feature fluency: the format is live but the creator economics are not, so early long-form on Instagram will be reruns unless someone funds originals. That is an opening for brands: fund Instagram-native series while creators are holding back, with negotiating room on distribution and creative control.

**L'Oréal skips the agency and makes its Urban Decay microdrama directly with ReelShort: 3.8M views in five days**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — L'Oréal's evolving microdrama strategy puts entertainment over product](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/loreals-evolving-microdrama-strategy-puts-entertainment-over-product/873646/), 5 Nov 2026
- **What:** "Ghost Hunter Hotline", six short episodes about getting revenge on people who ghost, built around Urban Decay's Ghosting Spray, launched 24 Oct and reached No. 2 on ReelShort's most-viewed ranking (brand-shared data). It took about four weeks. Unlike its 2025 Maybelline microdrama, L'Oréal produced it with the platform rather than an external agency, to use ReelShort's knowledge of the genre's audience. L'Oréal USA's CDMO frames it as audience-first, "entertainment first" marketing, and admits past bets on Periscope and BeReal did not pay off.
- **Why us:** A warning and an opening for us: platforms are now offering to make the work themselves. Our case for being in the room is cultural reading across platforms and making a series build into lasting brand memory, not one platform's formula. Follows the 10-15 Dentsu and 10-16 microdrama items; views are not sales.

**Ralph Lauren says marketing at 8.6% of sales "is not the ceiling"; Coach sells scarcity instead of discounts**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Luxury Briefing: Ralph Lauren is putting no ceiling on selling the marketing dream](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury/luxury-briefing-ralph-lauren-marketing-coach-black-friday/), 6 Nov 2026
- **What:** On its 5 Nov earnings call Ralph Lauren reported revenue +9.1% to $2.19B, marketing at 8.6% of sales (from 7.8%), holiday media +23%, holiday experiences in eight cities instead of four, and 1.7M new direct customers, with gifting framed as "an acquisition engine". Its first Polo Fest in Tokyo (23-25 Oct) put an archive exhibition next to a vintage marketplace run by collectors and dealers, with free tickets distributed through its LINE membership community. Coach (Tapestry call, 5 Nov) is cutting its most-discounted bags and plans to promote limited colourways on Black Friday; about 40% of its 2.1M new customers were Gen Z.
- **Why us:** Power arm, in the numbers: a luxury brand defends full price by spending more on the culture around the product, and by giving the collector community who kept the brand alive a stage. For luxury clients, community and collectors are the compounding asset; a discount spends the brand down. Follows the 10-15 Coach Tabby Tour item.

**Reddit's first People's Fashion Index: people crowdsource whether luxury is worth it**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Luxury Briefing (Reddit People's Fashion Index exclusive)](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury/luxury-briefing-ralph-lauren-marketing-coach-black-friday/), 6 Nov 2026 — quoting Matt Klein, Reddit's head of consumer insights
- **What:** Released 5 Nov, the index counts 193M US views of conversations about tracking down fashion in the year to 30 Sep (+17%), with exact-piece, celebrity and archival hunts making up 66% of them. Shoppers post screenshots of Vinted, Depop and Poshmark listings to ask both "is it real?" and "is it worth it in this condition?". Klein's line: an "invaluable premium on real lived experiences and human expertise". Reddit positions the report as a pitch to fashion brands, alongside its AI licensing deals and login tests (11-03).
- **Why us:** Think Forward: proof is moving from the brand to the crowd. Communities now decide authenticity and value before purchase, which is where a luxury premium is defended or lost (Proof feeding Power). It also gives Reddit, through Matt Klein (already on our thinker list), a concrete data-partner case for Think Forward.

## POVs

**Joellen Lu (OUAI, ex-Reformation): post smarter, not more, and build for social rather than adapt for it**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio — The GeoCities to social marketer pipeline](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/social-media-strategy-joellen-lu), 5 Nov 2026 — Rachel Karten with Joellen Lu
- **What:** Lu argues the era has moved from follower growth to views ("follower count is no longer the gatekeeper of reach") and that social is in a "hi-fi era". She prioritises views per post over total views, rewards community members whatever their audience size, and describes OUAI's horror-comedy series CHOPPED (with Victoria Pedretti) as social-first from day one, with costs rising sharply from in-house influencer content to a full SAG production. Prediction: as AI floods feeds, the clever commercial comes back and views become the new vanity metric.
- **Why us:** Agree and extend: "views per post" is a compounding metric (each post earns its place in brand memory), and "build for social, don't adapt for it" is our social-first case in a brand leader's words. Pairs with yesterday's Healey distinctiveness POV and the 10-28 Karten post on reach.

**GeoSurge: AI models choose which brands to search for from memory, so most AEO spend is aimed at the wrong end**  ·  `SIGNAL`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — How marketers can better optimize for AI search visibility](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/how-marketers-can-better-optimize-for-ai-search-visibility/833013/), 5 Nov 2026 — guest piece by Francisco Vigo, GeoSurge co-founder
- **What:** In the vendor's own test (66 buyer questions run 60 times each, about 13,000 model searches), brands the model already remembered were searched 55.7% of the time versus 17.4% for others; 31% of searches named a brand, and nearly two-thirds of those named one of the model's top-five remembered brands. The split varies: about 82% in automotive, about 50% in fitness and wellness. He separates retrievability (fixable with content) from memorability (built through coverage, reviews and category association over time).
- **Why us:** Supports yesterday's line that AEO is a Proof investment: memorability is brand-building by another name. Vendor-run study with small samples per industry, so cite as indicative only.
