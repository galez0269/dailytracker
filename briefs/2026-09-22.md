## Industry Buzz

**AI discovery splinters beyond ChatGPT**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Comscore data shows how AI discovery is splintering beyond ChatGPT](https://digiday.com/media/comscore-data-shows-how-ai-discovery-is-splintering-beyond-chatgpt/), Sept 22 2026
- **What:** ChatGPT's prompt-volume share fell from 70% to 50% in H1 2026 as Gemini and Claude both grew sharply; the more important gap is between being an AI's *source* and being its visible *citation* — a brand can be heavily used and still invisible.
- **Why us:** AEO strategy can no longer optimise for one assistant — worth a POV on measuring source-share across all three major engines, not just ChatGPT, before a client asks why their AI visibility looks fine on one dashboard and terrible on another.

**Creator budgets hit a holdco "toll booth"**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [LBB Online — Up to 65% of Creator Budgets Lost to Holdco 'Toll Booth': TrinityP3 Research](https://lbbonline.com/news/trinityp3-creator-marketing-budgets-industry-bulletin), Sept 21-22 2026
- **What:** TrinityP3's "Unmasking the Invisible Margin in Creator Marketing" bulletin finds holding companies inserting themselves as non-transparent middlemen between marketers and creators — in an aggregated, non-transparent model, as little as 35% of a $100k budget reaches the creator.
- **Why us:** A direct opening for "we know creators" as a genuinely differentiated, transparent alternative — this is exactly the practice a smaller, creator-first network can point to and say we don't do this.

**AI "parasite authors" impersonate real writers on Amazon**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [The Atlantic — The Rise of Parasite Authors](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/09/ai-authors-impersonating-writers-amazon/688709/), Sept 18 2026
- **What:** Romance author Tamra Westberry discovered an AI-generated novel listed under her pen name, reusing her characters and cover style and translated into four languages; a study of 14,000+ self-published Amazon genre novels found roughly one in five contained substantial AI-generated text.
- **Why us:** The starkest instance yet of unfakeable capital under direct attack — a creator's own name and style being counterfeited, not just their content copied; worth a position on creator-identity protection ahead of the Creator Report.
