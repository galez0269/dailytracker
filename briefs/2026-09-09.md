**Google won't be forced to break up its adtech business, despite a monopoly ruling**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`

- **Why us:** Reshapes the leverage question in programmatic/adtech economics without redrawing the market map — a live C-suite question on what actually changes for media buying and measurement now the ruling has landed.
