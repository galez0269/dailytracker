## Industry Buzz

**YouTube's Made On YouTube: AI feeds, live gamification, three-way A/B tests**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — YouTube invests in AI agents, live 'gamification,' and editing tools to keep creators close](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/youtube-invests-in-ai-agents-live-gamification-and-editing-tools-to-keep-creators-close/), Sept 25 2026
- **What:** Users can now curate their own homepage by interest via Gemini, and "Ask YouTube" folds shopping and image search into discovery; creators get live auto-dubbing, fan-gift "showdowns," a pre-publish test of three cuts with different pacing/tone, DMs, members-only communities and in-Studio pitch-deck tools.
- **Why us:** Textbook platform-feature fluency. User-curated feeds weaken the algorithm's hold on distribution, and communities plus DMs move value into owned relationships. Worth a fast client note on what changes in how we distribute and measure on YouTube this quarter.

**Meta's Muse: the ad business hiding inside an AI agent**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Future of Marketing Briefing: Imagining the ad business hiding inside Meta's Muse](https://digiday.com/marketing/future-of-marketing-briefing-imagining-the-ad-business-hiding-inside-metas-muse/), Sept 25 2026
- **What:** Muse (launched Sept 8) acts for users rather than feeding them content; it outpaced ChatGPT and Claude to 730,000 US downloads by day five. Likely monetisation is sponsored alternatives, placements inside machine-readable data, and merchant fees, all in tension with the trust that makes an agent useful.
- **Why us:** Extends the AEO thread into agentic territory. When the agent picks the answer, a brand's defence is being the obvious credible choice, which is unfakeable capital in commercial form. Frame it for clients before a platform sells them "sponsored recommendations."

**Creators move from brand deals to equity deals**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — From brand deals to equity deals: creators want a stake, not just a fee](https://digiday.com/media/from-brand-deals-to-equity-deals-creators-want-a-stake-not-just-a-fee/), Sept 24 2026
- **What:** Following Alix Earle's early Poppi stake, creators are taking sweat equity or angel positions. Platforms like Cherub and agencies like JERi are building the plumbing, and equity is framed as payment for strategic insight, not just posting.
- **Why us:** Direct Creator Report material. It also turns last week's TrinityP3 "toll booth" story around: creators are claiming more of the value, not less. "We know creators" should extend to advising brands on equity-grade creator partnerships.

**McDonald's doubles down on long-term brand building and launches a $1B media network ambition**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — Inside McDonald's new marketing model as it enters media network fray](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/inside-mcdonalds-new-marketing-model-as-it-enters-media-network-fray/831170/), Sept 23 2026

Its fans generate ~40% of positive QSR social mentions, more than its three nearest rivals combined.
- **Why us:** A top-tier FMCG proof point for the compounding thesis: a giant publicly choosing patient brand-building over spikes, with social fandom as the evidence. Flag for the FMCG Report.

**IAB Europe: 58% expect agentic ad buying at scale within a year**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Digiday — 58% of ad execs expect agentic buying to hit scale within a year, according to IAB Europe research](https://digiday.com/media-buying/58-of-ad-execs-expect-agentic-buying-to-hit-scale-within-a-year-according-to-iab-europe-research/), Sept 24 2026
- **What:** 86% of large (501+ staff) organisations already run advanced agentic systems vs 48% of smaller firms. Most deployments still keep humans in the loop and handle reporting and dashboards rather than decisions.
- **Why us:** C-suite ammunition on AI across the value chain. If buying automates, the differentiator shifts to the creative and cultural judgement agents can't supply.
