🆕 **Meta Q3: partnership ads pass a $15B run rate**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Meta Beats Q3 Expectations With Strong Earnings](https://www.adweek.com/media/meta-beats-q3-expectations-with-strong-earnings/), Oct 21 2026 (RSS date; no date on the page)
- **What:** Revenue was $64.6B, up 26%. Ad impressions rose 10% and the average price per ad rose 14%. Partnership ads, which brands run through creators' handles, grew more than 97% year on year and passed a $15B annual run rate. Nearly 10 million advertisers used at least one generative AI creative tool in the quarter. Meta credits its GEM ranking model with lifting app-ad conversions 9.6%. A $9.9B legal settlement charge cut GAAP operating income by 37%.
- **Why us:** Creator content run as paid media is now a major line on the biggest social platform. It's the scale proof behind the Kantar and Glossy findings and the Creator Report's "creator plus amplification" argument. With AI producing more and more of the ads, what's scarce is the credibility of a real creator, so the advantage goes to whoever picks the creators and shapes their content, not whoever produces the most assets.

**Kantar Media Reactions: confidence falls, AI and creators rise**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — How marketers can optimize their media mix as confidence takes a turn](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/how-marketers-can-optimize-their-media-mix-as-confidence-takes-a-turn/833197/), Oct 19 2026 (Kantar 2026 Media Reactions: 806 senior marketers, 23,452 consumers, 33 markets)
- **What:** 57% of advertisers are confident in their media mix, down from 64% a year ago. Only 50% are confident in their balance between brand and performance, down from 60%. Between 2020 and 2025, channels working together accounted for 43% of campaign performance, up from 18% before 2014. Kantar's line is that marketers are "measuring the instruments and not the orchestra". Nearly two-thirds of marketers expect generative AI to play a major role in brand recommendations, and 75% will increase investment in AI assistants, but only 23% of consumers trust AI's recommendations. In 2027 spending plans, micro creators are up 66%, nano 61%, macro 48% and mega 42%.
- **Why us:** The gap between how much marketers expect AI to drive recommendations and how few consumers trust them is our AEO argument: AI repeats the reputation brands earn in culture and with creators, it doesn't create one. The "orchestra" finding backs creators, paid and organic run as one system (platform feature fluency), not channel by channel. This is headline data for the Creator Report and Think Forward.

**Glossy's 2026 Creator Marketing Index: sponsored posts get the reach, gifted posts get the engagement**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Glossy+ Research 2026 Creator Marketing Index](https://www.glossy.co/research/glossy-research-2026-creator-marketing-index-a-brand-guide-to-effective-social-media-campaigns/), Oct 20 2026 (member-gated; Dash Social data)
- **What:** Glossy analysed 1,755 posts from 20 beauty and fashion campaigns. Sponsored posts averaged about 334K views against about 48K for gifted posts, but gifted demos out-engaged sponsored demos. Reels led Instagram engagement at 11%, against 7% for still posts and 5% for carousels. Charlotte Tilbury's #exaggereyes ranked No. 1 with 35% of its posts from gifted creators. 74 of the top 75 posts for Too Faced's #chocolatesoleil were organic. Big budgets topped the ranking, but Glossy notes that spend alone didn't guarantee results. 96% of brands now work with creators.
- **Why us:** It's evidence for a portfolio approach: paid creators for reach, seeded creators for credibility, and organic posts that carry the most Proof. That's the creator mix we build, and a ready-made data point for the Creator Report.

## POVs

🆕 **Eugene Healey: "the creator is a model of influence, not a job title"**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Considered Chaos — Post-Social Era :: Decentralised Influence](https://eugenehealey.substack.com/p/decentralised-influence), Oct 21 2026 — Eugene Healey
- **What:** Drawing on Edelman's 2026 Trust Barometer, Healey argues that trust has moved from institutions to individuals, so the single global brand narrative is ending. He says credibility now comes from conviction and having something at stake, not from credentials. His advice to brands is to spot people who already have influence and build around them rather than manufacture it, and to act as coordinator across many points of influence, including fans, rivals and communities. Examples include Tecate's "All In For Macario", Toyota's "Minivan Mom Stigma" and Doritos' "Think You Can Do Better?".
- **Why us:** We agree, and it's close to the Creator Report thesis, written by a voice clients read. "Recognise, don't manufacture" is unfakeable capital applied to creators, and "brand as coordinator" is Participation in the Loop. We should cite it or build on it, and make sure our report goes further with the operating model: how you run a network of points of influence at scale.

🆕 **Rachel Karten: fight over-optimisation with subversion**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio — Fight over-optimization with subversion](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/fight-over-optimization-with-subversion), Oct 20 2026 — Rachel Karten (paywalled beyond intro)
- **What:** Instagram and YouTube now offer AI assistants that coach creators, and AI-optimised feeds risk flattening content into a uniform middle. Karten argues that brands which deliberately break audience expectations will stand out. The paywalled part covers examples from PBS, Reformation, the Buffalo Bills, Ramp and Visit Oslo's "anti-ad".
- **Why us:** It counterbalances the Meta and AI-tools story. If every advertiser has the same optimisation engine, distinctiveness is the edge, which is "ideas worth talking about" in practice. Pair it with Healey for Think Forward: AI handles optimisation, while culture and conviction set brands apart.

**Matt Klein's field guide to cultural strategy: stay ahead without burning out**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [ZINE — How To Stay Ahead (& Sane) in Cultural Strategy](https://zine.kleinkleinklein.com/p/how-to-stay-ahead-and-sane), Oct 19 2026 — Matt Klein (paywalled beyond intro and survey)
