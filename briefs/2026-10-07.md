## Industry Buzz

**Apple's dynamic tracker blocklist hits the open web**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Apple's secret blocklist is the talk of Advertising Week New York (mostly off the record)](https://digiday.com/media-buying/apples-secret-blocklist-is-the-talk-of-advertising-week-new-york-mostly-off-the-record/), Oct 7 2026
- **What:** iOS 27 (mid-September) cut Safari off from cross-site tracking companies. The first names blocked include The Trade Desk, LiveRamp, ID5, Permutive and Experian's Audigent, while rivals such as LiveIntent, Lotame and Yahoo ConnectID got through in a test of 70+ domains. Apple requires every iPhone and iPad browser to run on WebKit, so Chrome and Firefox are affected too. New WebKit code (not yet shipped) will let Apple add companies dynamically without a software update. One exec's view: "a slow death" for ID companies, with vendors moving their pixels server-side.
- **Why us:** Open-web targeting keeps losing signal, and platform-native, logged-in environments (social, retail media, creators' own audiences) gain from that. The C-suite version: reach you rent through third-party IDs is getting weaker, and presence you earn on platforms is getting stronger. It's another reason to build first-party community, a compounding asset Apple can't switch off.

**Creators move to the centre of brand creative**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — How brands' deeper creator deals are unlocking cost efficiencies, results](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/how-brands-deeper-creator-deals-are-unlocking-cost-efficiencies-results/832205/), Oct 6 2026 (also [Adweek — Dear Media and Amanda Hirsch Want Brands To Ditch the Script](https://www.adweek.com/media/dear-media-amanda-hirsch-advertising-week/), Oct 6)
- **What:** Chili's says influencers are part of every tentpole and all of that content feeds paid media, where it used to run TV cutdowns. A no-frills mukbang with creator Keila Pacheco "performed so much better" than its Lizzo spot, and influencer work took the Triple Dipper from 7% to 15% of sales. It's also changing how the chain develops new menu items. Alaska Airlines finds long-term creator contracts cheaper and closer to how creators want to work. Disney redeploys creators across films, parks and D23. AWNY expected more than twice as many creator delegates as in 2025. Dear Media (about 100 shows, rarely programmatic) says the biggest brand mistake is still handing talent a script.
- **Why us:** Clear Creator Report evidence that creators now shape creative strategy, not just distribution. Chili's is the proof point: creator content leading paid media and moving a share of sales. Long-term creator rosters are a compounding model, which is our wheelhouse. Pair it with yesterday's Glossy "off-script" roundtable: the market is now saying "let creators cook" from every side.

## Trends

**Gen Alpha: "We want real"**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday (via Modern Retail) — From influencers to AI, here's what Gen Alpha thinks about your brand](https://digiday.com/marketing/from-influencers-to-ai-heres-what-gen-alpha-thinks-about-your-brand/), Oct 7 2026
- **What:** Five Gen Alpha shoppers on a Shoptalk Fall panel rejected AI in ads outright ("a huge turnoff"). They want real people as representatives, and say they trust a brand only as much as the person fronting it. Some research supply chains, and one said it would take two years to trust a brand again after greenwashing. The cohort influences nearly half of household spending in families with 8–14s and has about $100B in direct spending power.
- **Why us:** The third time in recent weeks that Gen Alpha has used "AI" as a byword for fake (Sept 29 "that's AI"), so it's now a pattern, not an anecdote. Unfakeable capital in the next generation's own words. Our line on embedding AI should be clear: AI behind the work, humans and creators in front of it.

**Borrowed prestige doubles mass fashion's media value**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Glossy — Zara and Gap are borrowing luxury's playbook. Can they keep the attention?](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/zara-gap-luxury-marketing-launchmetrics/), Oct 7 2026
- **What:** Launchmetrics' "Desirability Gap" report finds that the top 50 mass-market brands using premium strategies (designer partnerships, ambassadors, cultural events) averaged twice the media impact value of those that didn't. Zara's Galliano announcement drove $8.3M MIV in 48 hours, with "archival authority" the dominant theme, and Bad Bunny's Super Bowl look drove $19.9M. Gap's Zac Posen line rides alongside +10% comparable sales. Launchmetrics' CMO says longevity depends on what the brand reinforces next.
- **Why us:** The Loop's Power arm, measured. Borrowed authority buys attention, and only sustained reinforcement turns it into Power, which is the compounding argument applied to luxury codes. It's useful for the luxury-fashion secondary category.
