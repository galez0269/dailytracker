## Industry Buzz

**Instagram "Features": long-form horizontal video, plus Reels that link into it**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio — Instagram goes long-form with Features](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/instagram-goes-long-form-with-features), 26 Oct 2026; [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/instagram-is-going-long-but-will-audiences-stick-around), 28 Oct 2026
- **What:** Instagram is testing a long-form horizontal format (reported at up to 90 minutes, possibly viewable on TV) as part of an entertainment push, and creators can attach multiple Reels to one long video so short clips feed the long piece. Open questions flagged by both: how the recommendation algorithm treats long video and whether brands should treat it as extra ad space. Follows the 10-15 test of recommending 3-minute-plus Reels to non-followers; only the two sources above report the 90-minute figure so far.
- **Why us:** Platform feature fluency in real time: we can be first to say how long-form changes creator briefs (Reels as trailers for a series, not a standalone) and to test recuts on client channels this week; also relevant to the microdrama and entertainment-as-budget-line thread.

**McDonald's exits FIFA after 32 years, shifts to year-round, fan-first, own-IP programming**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/what-mcdonalds-dropping-its-fifa-sponsorship-means-for-sports-marketing/872775/), 27 Oct 2026; corroborated by [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mcdonald-s-ends-32-year-fifa-world-cup-partnership), 27 Oct 2026
- **What:** McDonald's committed nearly $80M to its largest-ever World Cup campaign (100+ markets); Kantar data showed lifts in NPS and youth social buzz, but Q2 traffic fell, the company attributed about a third of that dip to the campaign, and its CFO called it underperformance. It is moving to smaller, distributed partnerships (Ligue 1, a Chicago stadium naming deal, F1 in LatAm, hundreds of athlete deals).
- **Why us:** A live counter-example to the "spend in spikes" model and a quotable data point for the Q2 compounding thesis; the activation-cost point (rights fee is the cheap part) is a Think Forward and FMCG Report argument for owned, creator-led, always-on presence.

**Clorox moves from brand campaigns to "systems of relevance"**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/why-clorox-shifted-from-creating-brand-campaigns-to-systems-of-relevance/838232/), 26 Oct 2026 (CMO Eric Schwartz at ANA Masters of Marketing, 22 Oct)
- **What:** Always-on listen-test-optimise system built on in-house "brand lions", AI that adjusts live creative, and agency partners. Brand-reported results: Pine-Sol 150+ organic posts a month, 13% engagement and TikTok Shop limited editions selling out in hours; Brita 100+ assets a month, 1,742% year-on-year organic impressions; Hidden Valley 6B+ impressions off a TSA-post response. All figures are Clorox's own.
- **Why us:** Direct FMCG Report material and a Proof play for the Loop: consistent native presence with AI behind the work and humans out front. Also the sharpest "what does social-first FMCG look like in practice" case for the C-suite AI-in-value-chain question.

**Holdco swap and build-out: Omnicom takes Coke NA media, Publicis builds creator and culture into Marcel**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — Omnicom-IPG deal "substantially completed" as group wins Coke's NA media](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/omnicom-ipg-deal-substantially-completed-as-group-wins-cokes-na-media/833837/), 23 Oct 2026; [Marketing Dive — Publicis installs leadership atop Marcel](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/publicis-installs-leadership-atop-marcel-as-agentic-drives-growth-plans/872691/), 26 Oct 2026
- **What:** Coke has moved North American media to Omnicom after Publicis won PepsiCo global media without a pitch; Omnicom says the IPG integration is nearly done ($900M savings target) with integrated media up 10%+. Publicis is giving its Marcel AI platform a CEO and CSO and extending it into influencer and creator marketing, sports, culture and AI-chatbot visibility.
- **Why us:** Extends the 10-15 holdco-to-CEO thread: Publicis is now packaging creators, culture and AEO inside a data platform, which is our wheelhouse. Our counter is first-hand creator and cultural fluency; the Coke/Pepsi swap is a FMCG-client context line.

**AEO moves from "get cited" to "be believed": earned media and proof surfaces decide who is chosen**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing-Interactive — Your brand made it into the AI answer. Now what?](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/your-brand-made-it-into-the-ai-answer-now-what), 27 Oct 2026 (Forrester AEO report); [WARC — The Validation Economy (Cal McCahon, Croud)](https://www.warc.com/en/article/the-validation-economy-ai-shapes-the-shortlist-but-people-decide-who-to-trust-ba8ce90877f941f0b008a3a917d1f058), 26 Oct 2026
- **What:** Forrester argues being surfaced is not being chosen; cited figures include roughly 85-90% of AI-answer citations coming from earned media (Ogilvy) and trust rising about fivefold when a recognised publisher is cited (WSJ Intelligence). WARC's Croud piece frames the risk as appearing in the AI shortlist but looking weak on the surfaces shoppers check next.
- **Why us:** Answers the LLM-visibility C-suite hot button with a through-line we own: creator and earned-media proof is what models cite and people verify. Extends the 10-15 "AI visibility becomes brand strategy" must-know; Think Forward material.

**Meta delays its new commercial terms to 20 Nov after publisher and advertiser pushback**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/meta-delays-updated-commercial-terms-amid-advertiser-concerns), 26 Oct 2026
- **What:** The updated terms make businesses accountable for Meta AI output, bar removal of AI watermarks and metadata, and grant Meta broad rights to published content and crawling; complaints have reached EU and UK antitrust regulators. The effective date moves from 30 Oct to 20 Nov.
- **Why us:** Client-facing and time-boxed: every brand running Meta AI creative tools should know what they are signing. Fits our platform-feature fluency position, and the AI-watermark clause matters for any provably-human positioning.

## POVs

**Eugene Healey — "The Control Crisis": brand guidelines no longer give control, so adopt a guerrilla mindset**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Considered Chaos](https://eugenehealey.substack.com/p/the-control-crisis), 28 Oct 2026 — Eugene Healey
- **What:** Brand meaning now reaches audiences via creators, collaborations and AI search, so airtight guidelines cannot govern every touchpoint. He argues for a brand as a canvas others build on (Lidl's Lidlize, GoDaddy), with the system and a few boundaries held and the executions given away.
- **Why us:** Extends our argument: counter is "control the pattern, not the post" (the Loop's Presence needs coherence; Participation needs room). Another peer making the compounding-through-creators case, so the whitespace is the operating model, not the diagnosis.

**Zoe Scaman — "The Tithe": fans pay for belonging with visible effort, and AI makes effort unverifiable**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Musings Of A Wandering Mind](https://zoescaman.substack.com/p/the-tithe), 26 Oct 2026 — Zoe Scaman (adapted from a Popcultr Marketing Summit talk)
- **What:** Fandom runs on effort made visible; where AI replaces that effort, fans punish it (she cites Black Ops 7 refunds and a 2026 survey in which 97.8% avoid suspected-AI work) but tolerate it behind the scenes. Her playbook: map each fan activity, use AI behind the work, give fans AI-assisted instruments not generators, disclose.
- **Why us:** Unfakeable capital with a usable rule for brands: AI behind the work, humans where the effort is paid. Direct Think Forward material and a ready answer to the AI-in-value-chain question for entertainment clients.

**Eugene Healey — "Decentralised Influence": trust moves to individuals with skin in the game**  ·  `SIGNAL`

- **Why us:** Companion to today's Control Crisis post; a creator-led brand framing we already practise.

**Contagious / Link in Bio — "The new rules of social media marketing"**  ·  `SIGNAL`
