## Industry Buzz

**Meta's Q2 earnings show the AI-spend/margin trade-off, live**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Meta's Tepid Revenue Outlook Undercuts its AI Spending Spree](https://www.adweek.com/media/metas-tepid-revenue-outlook-undercuts-its-ai-spending-spree/), Jul 29–30 2026
- **What:** Q2 revenue hit $60.8bn (+28% YoY), but net profit fell as legal charges, layoff severance, and a raised $130–145bn full-year CapEx guide compress margins; Zuckerberg is framing AI agents as a future paid-services layer, not yet a return.

**X and WFA end the two-year GARM/advertiser-boycott lawsuit**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Adweek — Elon Musk's X and WFA End Multiyear Legal Spat](https://www.adweek.com/media/elon-musks-x-and-wfa-end-multiyear-legal-spat/), Jul 29 2026
- **What:** X's antitrust suit against the World Federation of Advertisers over the GARM brand-safety coalition is settled; WFA won't revive GARM or a similar body. Financial terms undisclosed.
- **Why us:** The collective brand-safety watchdog model is gone — advertiser trust in platforms now has to be built brand-by-brand, not policed by an industry body. That's the Proof arm of the Loop and the unfakeable-capital argument in one story.
