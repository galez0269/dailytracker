## Industry Buzz

**Amazon Ads Agent: agentic campaign buying arrives, with transparency questions attached**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Digiday — Amazon's Ads nears $20 billion in Q3 as it bets on agentic AI](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazons-ads-nears-20-billion-in-q3-as-it-bets-on-agentic-ai-to-fuel-further-growth/), 30 Oct 2026; [Adweek — Amazon says external AI agents aren't hurting its ad business](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/amazon-says-external-ai-agents-arent-hurting-its-ad-business/), 29 Oct 2026 (date from Adweek feed; body paywalled)
- **What:** Amazon's Q3 ad revenue was roughly $19.6B (Digiday reports +12%, Adweek +11%; a Prime Day timing shift distorts the quarter-on-quarter view). The new Ads Agent folds sponsored, display, video and audio into one prompt-driven flow (planning, creative, forecasting, optimisation, measurement); Amazon claims beta gains of up to 67% higher long-term return on ad spend and 29% lower new-to-brand cost, which are its own figures. Analysts read it as a play for smaller advertisers and agency-held upper-funnel budget; sources question whether advertisers can inspect or challenge the AI's decisions, with the FTC and 22 state AGs already suing over alleged hidden auction surcharges.
- **Why us:** Hits two C-suite buttons at once: embedding AI across the value chain (where do agencies add value when the platform plans and buys itself?) and new platform features that change how we distribute and measure. Our angle is the part an agent cannot automate: creator and cultural fluency feeding the creative the agent optimises. Pairs with the 07-31 Sponsored AI Prompts must-know.

**Google's AI Mode monetises below classic search; shoppers are already moving to AI chat**  ·  `MUST-KNOW`
- **Source:** [Marketing Dive — Google's AI overhaul of search hits snags](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/googles-ai-overhaul-of-search-hits-snags-as-ad-revenue-growth-cools/873150/), 29 Oct 2026; [WARC — For shoppers, AI chat now beats traditional search](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/for-shoppers-ai-chat-now-beats-traditional-search-dd86f65c0f0f4944b409b00e7af53aa4), 29 Oct 2026 (headline and listing summary only; body gated); [Adweek — Your Marketing Playbook Expired Last Week ft. - **What:** Google's search ads grew 12% and YouTube ads 9% in Q3 (15% each a year ago); search still brought in $63.4B and query growth was the fastest in five years, but the CBO tied the revenue gap to lower monetisation in AI Mode. Salesforce's CMO says on Adweek's podcast that AEO is its own discipline, not a rebranded SEO (page lists it as a takeaway, no reasoning visible).
- **Why us:** Extends the AEO thread from "get cited" (07-30) and "be believed" (10-28) to "who gets paid": attention is moving to AI answers before the ad model catches up, which makes earned and creator-backed proof, not ad slots, the durable route in. ## POVs

**Rachel Karten / Amy Francombe — "Short-form audiences are rented attention": depth of audience beats reach**  ·  `HIGH-IMPACT`
- **Source:** [Link in Bio](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/short-form-audiences-are-rented-attention), 29 Oct 2026 — guest essay by Amy Francombe
- **What:** Short-form virality does not create loyalty; long-form is harder to fake and builds trust, so follower counts and engagement rates miss real influence. Evidence: a small creator at 15% engagement against about 0.3% for a million-follower account (one strategist's example), Coach's long-form YouTube campaign (15M+ organic engagements, +60% Gen Z top-of-mind, sixfold consideration; brand-reported), Figma and GM/Cadillac series. Eugene Healey is quoted on long-form reflecting creator effort. The call with Instagram's VP of Product the day before is mentioned, but the essay reports nothing specific from it.
- **Why us:** Agree and extend: it is the audience-side case for Instagram's Features test and for compounding over reach. The whitespace is measurement: what do we count if followers and views are rented? A natural Creator Report and platform-fluency point.

**Vijay Raj (ex-Unilever insights) on WARC — treat creators as "similar but different"**  ·  `SIGNAL`
- **Source:** [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/article/why-marketers-need-to-treat-creators-as-similar-but-different-13eb42a7648b452a864a55d6aa949be1), 28 Oct 2026 — Vijay Raj (WARC; gated, standfirst only)
